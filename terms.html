The final price, contract length and all terms of your rental are those in Coway's official order summary and rental agreement. If anything on this website differs, Coway's documents prevail.

1. The current promotion

  • Promotion prices (marked * or shown in red) are introductory monthly rates for selected products and approved applications only.
  • The introductory rate applies for the first few months, depending on the product and plan. The normal monthly rate applies after that.
  • The promotion runs until the date Coway sets or while stocks last, and Coway may change or withdraw it without notice.
  • Gifts or extra benefits, if any, depend on eligibility and availability.

2. Rental plans

Rental is subject to approval by Coway (M) Sdn Bhd. The monthly rental, contract period, payment method and early termination charges are stated in your order summary. No deposit is needed for the listed rental plans unless Coway states otherwise.

3. Who does what

  • an independent Coway Health Planner (independent Health Planner): product advice, quotation and order submission to Coway.
  • Coway (M) Sdn Bhd: order approval, delivery, installation, regular servicing, filter replacement and warranty.

4. Delivery and installation

Delivery and installation timing depends on stock, approval and your location. Next-day installation is not guaranteed.

5. Cancellation

Cancellation, refunds and early termination follow Coway's rental agreement for your plan.

6. Prices on this website

Prices are in Ringgit Malaysia (RM) and are for reference. We update them regularly, but please confirm the latest price with us before you order.

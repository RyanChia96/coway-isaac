## Takeaways

- **WordPress dominates:** 35 of 53 live sites. Our static site will load faster than most of them, which helps Google Ads Quality Score.
- **10 sites show a Google Ads tag in their HTML** and 19 use Tag Manager, so at least 10 are clearly running Google Ads. Expect competition on keywords like "coway promotion", "penapis air coway" and "coway water purifier".
- **Many domains use "official" in the name** (cowayofficial.com, coway-official.com, mycoway-official.com, cowayofficial.my, coway-officialsales.com) or copy Coway's own page title ("Coway Malaysia Official Site"). Google can reject ads for that under its misrepresentation policy. Our "independent Health Planner" wording keeps us clear of it.
- **Malay-language sites are a big share** (cowaypakej.com, ejencoway.my, cowayofficial.com, daftarcowaymalaysia.com, promositerkini.my, cowaypromoterbaru.my). A Bahasa Malaysia version of our page would reach searches most English-only competitors miss.
- **Stale promos are common:** many sites still show 2024–2025 or already-ended 2026 promotions. Keeping our promo month current in `js/config.js` is an easy edge.
- **Current offer being advertised:** RM20/month for the first 3–7 months, ending 26 November 2026, per agent site coway-my.my. Not checked against Coway's own site — confirm with your agent.

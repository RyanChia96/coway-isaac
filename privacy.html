This website is operated by an independent, authorised Coway Health Planner, (SSM ), agent ID . It is not the official website of Coway (M) Sdn Bhd.

1. What we collect

When you contact us through WhatsApp or the quote form, we may receive your name, phone number, area and the product you are interested in. The quote form does not store your answers on a server: it opens WhatsApp with your message written out, and you choose whether to send it.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and recommend a product and plan.
  • To submit your order to Coway (M) Sdn Bhd if you decide to go ahead.
  • To follow up on your order, installation and after-sales questions.
  • To measure and improve the website and our advertising, only with your consent.

3. Who we share it with

We share your details with Coway (M) Sdn Bhd only to process your order, delivery, installation, servicing and warranty. We do not sell your personal data. Advertising and measurement data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet legal obligations, then delete them.

5. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), you may ask to see or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent for us to contact you. Send the request to us on WhatsApp.

6. Cookies

With your consent, we use Google Ads and Google Analytics cookies to measure visits and which ads lead to an enquiry. Until you accept, these tags run in a cookieless mode that stores nothing on your device. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie notice on the homepage.
